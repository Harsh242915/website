## Win sequence

A win reveals each payline, then shows a counting award with coin particles and a musical arpeggio. Tiers: Big Win at 10× bet, Mega Win at 25×, Epic Win at 50×. Multiple rows below 10× show Multi Win. Collect, Enter, Space or Escape finishes the presentation; the underlying credit award is applied once. Preview buttons leave credits unchanged.
